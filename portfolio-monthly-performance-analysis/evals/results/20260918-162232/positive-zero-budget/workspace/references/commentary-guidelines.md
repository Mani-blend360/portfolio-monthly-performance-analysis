# Commentary guidelines

Write concise, factual commentary for investment and portfolio-management stakeholders. Lead with the few material movements defining the month. Contrast baselines explicitly; do not collapse “below budget but above prior year” into one judgment.

Use units on numbers and distinguish `%`, percentage points, and bps. For a zero baseline say “percentage variance not meaningful.” Describe threshold equality as material.

Pair each movement only with a supported driver. Keep hypotheses separate. Strong order intake with weak revenue is a contrast, not proof of conversion delays. Questions should request a missing bridge, quantified impact, evidence, owner, or timing. Risks and opportunities are observations, not recommendations. Include only supplied actions.

Before reporting `PASS`, verify all figures against calculation JSON, materiality boundary rules, direction rules, zero handling, evidence tags, and management questions for every material unexplained movement.
