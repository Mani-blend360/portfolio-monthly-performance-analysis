# Materiality and favorability

Thresholds are configured per row. `absolute` uses absolute variance magnitude; `percent` uses percentage variance magnitude and makes zero comparisons `not_assessable`; `bps` uses basis-point difference magnitude. Equality counts as material.

Mechanical favorability follows direction: positive is favorable for `higher_is_favorable`; negative is favorable for `lower_is_favorable`; zero and all `neutral` rules are neutral. Missing comparisons are `not_assessable`. Mechanical favorability is not an investment conclusion and may need business context.
